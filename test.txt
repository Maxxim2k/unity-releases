Compatible with firmware >= 3.5.x (no wire-protocol changes).

##### Changed:
- Dashboard cards and the self-serve tile: the state badge (READY, FLOATING and so on) now sits to the right of the tank name.
- Light, audio and volume bubbles at the top of every dashboard card, the self-serve tile and the tank page, the same size as the state badge: LIGHT ON / LIGHT OFF always, and AUDIO PLAYING with VOLUME MUTE / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH while music plays in a session. The small light and audio chips under the tank picture are gone.
- Float time and temperature rows show the unit with the label ("Float time (min)", "Float temperature (°F)") instead of inside the number box, on the dashboard and the tank page.

##### Fixed:
- During the intro track the card no longer shows a wrong "5 min remaining". The tank does not count down in the intro or exit track; the card now says "float starts after the intro" or "exit music playing", and the tank page's TIME LEFT shows a dash.
- TURN LIGHT ON / OFF on the dashboard now says "TURNING LIGHT ON…" / "TURNING LIGHT OFF…" until the tank reports the change (8 seconds at most), and the LIGHT ON / OFF bubble shows the light's state.
