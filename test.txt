Compatible with firmware >= 3.5.x. The countdowns, the pump test and filter-only mode need tank firmware 4.0.4 or later; on older firmware the screens explain the problem without them.

##### Added:
- A flow switch problem now shows a solid red two-line bar on the dashboard that everyone sees, self-serve guests included. For a switch stuck ON it says how many days the tank can keep running and, in large letters, DO NOT TURN OFF THE PUMP OVERRIDE SWITCH — MAY CAUSE DAMAGE. The tank's card shows the same in orange.
- Tapping the bar (or FLOW SWITCH HELP on the tank screen) opens a new flow switch screen with photos of the pump override switch, the control box plugs, the flow switch and the filter pressure gauge, a live pump and switch reading, and step-by-step checks.
- No flow: a 5-minute pump test (staff only). The pump runs alone, with heaters and UV off, and stops by itself once the switch sees water. If the switch never sees water, staff can choose WATER IS MOVING — SWITCH IS BROKEN to filter without the flow switch for 7 days: in-tank heaters only, inline heater and UV off during cleaning. This replaces the 2-hour flow bypass.
- A green FLOW SWITCH WORKING bar for a minute once a fixed switch reads normally.
- Pump schedule: a 24 HOURS button keeps the pump off all day in Idle and heats with the in-tank heaters only.

##### Changed:
- CLEAR FAULT is gone. A power cycle resets the flow switch fault; replacing the switch always involves one.
- The flow switch fault message and checklists now say "do not turn OFF the pump override switch", and the no-flow checklist checks the override switch first, then the filter gauge (above 13 psi).
- Tank screen Flow switch row: "Testing pump" during the pump test, FAILED – STUCK OFF in filter-only mode.
